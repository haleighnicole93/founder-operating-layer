# Founder Operating Layer

**Simulated Executive Operations Project**

An AI-assisted executive operating system designed to turn a founder's constant stream of meetings, requests, decisions, personal logistics, and follow-up into a clear view of what actually requires their attention.

## The Problem

In an early-stage company, a founder can become the default destination for nearly every question, decision, request, and problem.

The challenge isn't simply organizing the volume. It's determining:

- What genuinely requires founder judgment
- What can be handled independently
- What should be delegated or coordinated
- What can wait or be declined
- What context the founder needs to make a decision quickly
- What downstream effects need to be resolved after a decision is made

A full calendar and an empty inbox aren't the goal. Protecting founder attention while keeping the business moving is.

## The Approach

This system uses AI for information processing and human judgment for decision-making.

AI can help summarize inputs, extract deadlines, identify conflicts, retrieve context, and prepare routine drafts.

The Executive Partner remains responsible for understanding relationships, business impact, timing, executive preferences, and the consequences of each decision.

> **Operating principle:** The founder should see the right things at the right time, with enough context to act quickly, while everything else continues moving.

## What's Inside

This repository demonstrates the operating logic behind the system through a simulated high-growth startup scenario.

- **Sample Incoming Queue:** Competing business and personal requests arriving simultaneously
- **Priority Framework:** How each input is evaluated before reaching the founder
- **Founder Brief:** What the founder sees after the original queue has been processed
- **Decision Log:** Decisions requiring founder judgment and their downstream actions
- **Follow-Through Tracker:** Owners, deadlines, dependencies, and escalation points
- **AI + Human Workflow:** Where AI assists and where human judgment remains essential
