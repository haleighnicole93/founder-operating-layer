# Sample Incoming Queue

## Scenario

It’s 8:05 AM at a fast-growing, 12-person AI startup.

Overnight and early-morning activity has created competing demands across investors, candidates, product, customers, travel, the team, and the founder’s personal life.

The founder also has two hours of protected product-strategy time today. The following inputs arrive before the day is fully underway.

---

### 1. Product Launch
Today's 3:00 PM launch is blocked. The product team needs a founder pricing decision by 10:00 AM.

### 2. Investor Request
An existing investor requests 30 minutes this week regarding an introduction to a potential strategic partner. No immediate deadline.

### 3. Final-Round Candidate
A high-priority engineering candidate can only interview today from 2:30–3:00 PM, during the founder's protected focus block.

### 4. Customer Escalation
A major customer asks the founder to join a 1:00 PM call regarding concerns about an upcoming product change.

### 5. Travel Disruption
The founder's 7:40 PM flight is canceled. They must attend an investor meeting tomorrow morning.

### 6. Board Member Email
A board member sends three questions about tomorrow's update and requests a response today.

### 7. Team Approval Request
A team member requests founder approval for a routine software purchase already within the approved budget.

### 8. Introduction Request
An acquaintance requests a 30-minute coffee next week without a clear business purpose.

### 9. Personal Logistics
The founder's dinner reservation conflicts with available replacement flights.

### 10. Missing Meeting Prep
Tomorrow morning's investor meeting has no finalized pre-read. Two team members still owe inputs.

### 11. Vendor Issue
A recurring vendor says an overdue invoice must be resolved today to prevent service interruption.

### 12. Protected Focus Time
The founder has 2:00–4:00 PM blocked for uninterrupted product strategy work. It is the only available focus block this week.

---

## The Challenge

These inputs are intentionally different.

Some are urgent but do not require the founder. Some involve important relationships without immediate deadlines. Some require founder judgment, while others should be resolved before they ever reach the founder.

The objective is not to process the queue in the order it arrived.

The objective is to determine what deserves founder attention, what can move without them, and what secondary problems each decision creates.
