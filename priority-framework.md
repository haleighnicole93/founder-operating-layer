# Priority Framework

The goal of triage is not to determine which request sounds the most urgent. It is to determine what actually requires founder attention and what can continue moving without it.

## Decision Questions

Every incoming request is evaluated against six questions.

### 1. Does this actually require the founder?
Is their judgment, authority, relationship, or decision uniquely necessary?

### 2. What is the business impact?
What moves, stalls, or becomes more difficult if this isn't addressed?

### 3. What is the real deadline?
Urgent-sounding and actually urgent aren't always the same thing.

### 4. Can I resolve it independently?
If I have enough context and authority to handle it, it shouldn't consume founder attention.

### 5. Can someone else move it forward first?
Gather inputs, resolve dependencies, and bring the founder a decision instead of a problem.

### 6. What are the downstream effects?
Solving one problem shouldn't quietly create three more.

---

## Routing

After evaluation, each item follows one of four routes.

### NEEDS FOUNDER
Requires founder judgment, authority, relationship, or a decision.

The objective is to present the founder with enough context to make the decision quickly, not forward the original problem.

### I HANDLE
Can be resolved independently without consuming founder attention.

This includes resolving the immediate issue and any downstream effects created by the solution.

### DELEGATE / COORDINATE
Someone else owns the input or execution. I own making sure it gets done.

That means identifying the owner, establishing a deadline, tracking dependencies, following up, and escalating only when necessary.

### DEFER / DECLINE
Doesn't justify founder attention or calendar space right now.

Open calendar space is not automatically available time.

## Applied Judgment Examples

### Product Launch
**Route: NEEDS FOUNDER**

The pricing decision is blocking a same-day launch and requires founder authority.

Before escalating, gather the available options, the product team's recommendation, relevant tradeoffs, and the actual decision deadline.

**Founder receives:** A decision with context, not a forwarded Slack message.

### Final-Round Candidate
**Route: NEEDS FOUNDER + COORDINATE**

First verify whether 2:30 PM is truly the candidate's only option.

If it is, evaluate the strategic value of the hire against the protected focus block. For a critical early hire, recommend using 30 minutes while protecting the remaining 90.

**Principle:** Protected time matters because of what it enables. Protect the purpose, not simply the calendar block.

### Customer Escalation
**Route: COORDINATE → NEEDS FOUNDER only if necessary**

Before committing founder time, get context from the account owner:

- What happened?
- What is the relationship or revenue risk?
- Who can resolve it?
- Why has founder involvement been requested?
- Would founder presence materially change the outcome?

Founder involvement follows the assessment rather than the request.

### Travel Disruption
**Route: I HANDLE**

Identify viable replacement flights, account for arrival time and tomorrow's commitments, rebook within known preferences and authority, and update ground transportation.

Then resolve the downstream conflict with dinner.

**Principle:** Rebooking the flight while leaving three new problems behind isn't actually solving the problem.

### Board Questions
**Route: DELEGATE / COORDINATE → NEEDS FOUNDER where necessary**

Route each question to the appropriate subject-matter owner, gather the inputs, and consolidate the response.

Only the portions requiring founder judgment should consume founder time.

### Routine Software Approval
**Route: I HANDLE**

If the purchase is within an approved budget and existing authority allows it, process it without escalation.

If routine purchases repeatedly require founder approval despite existing budget authority, flag the workflow for improvement.

### Investor Pre-Read
**Route: DELEGATE / COORDINATE**

Give outstanding owners a specific deadline and ask them to surface blockers before that deadline.

Track completion and escalate only if the deliverable becomes genuinely at risk.

The founder receives the assembled pre-read, not a running commentary about chasing inputs.

---

## Operating Principle

> Urgent does not automatically mean important. Important does not automatically mean the founder needs to handle it.

Executive partnership means understanding the difference.
