# AI + Human Workflow

## The Principle

AI can make executive support faster without making executive judgment automatic.

In this system, AI handles the first layer of information processing. The Executive Partner remains responsible for context, judgment, routing, escalation, and execution.

> **Human-in-the-loop principle:** AI should make it faster to understand the situation. Human judgment determines what happens next.

---

## 1. CAPTURE

Information enters from sources such as:

- Email
- Slack
- Calendar
- Meeting notes
- Travel alerts
- Internal requests
- External stakeholders
- Personal logistics

The objective is to create visibility across inputs without requiring the founder to monitor every source personally.

---

## 2. PROCESS WITH AI

AI can assist with high-volume information processing.

### Summarize incoming information

Condense messages, calendar changes, meeting notes, and other inputs into actionable context.

### Extract deadlines and dependencies

Identify dates, owners, blockers, commitments, and unresolved questions.

### Detect conflicts

Flag overlapping meetings, travel disruptions, missing preparation, and scheduling constraints.

### Retrieve context

Surface related communications, prior decisions, meeting history, and relevant documents for review.

### Prepare routine drafts

Draft acknowledgments, scheduling messages, follow-ups, and status updates for human review.

---

## 3. APPLY HUMAN JUDGMENT

AI output informs the decision. It does not make the decision.

The Executive Partner evaluates:

- Business impact
- Whether founder involvement is uniquely necessary
- Relationship context
- Actual urgency
- Executive preferences
- Existing authority
- Strategic priorities
- Downstream effects

### Human judgment determines:

**What deserves founder attention**

Urgency alone does not determine importance.

**Whether the founder is uniquely necessary**

A request for founder involvement does not automatically justify founder involvement.

**What gets protected**

Focus time, relationships, strategic work, and personal commitments require context AI may not fully understand.

**When to act independently**

If sufficient context and authority exist, the issue should be resolved without unnecessary escalation.

**When circumstances change the rules**

A critical hire, major customer relationship, investor issue, or emerging risk may justify overriding the normal process.

---

## 4. ROUTE

After human review, each input follows one of four paths:

### NEEDS FOUNDER
Prepare the decision, context, recommendation, and deadline.

### I HANDLE
Resolve independently, including downstream effects.

### DELEGATE / COORDINATE
Assign or identify the appropriate owner, establish timing, track dependencies, and follow through.

### DEFER / DECLINE
Protect founder time from work that does not currently justify attention.

---

## 5. EXECUTE + FOLLOW THROUGH

Triage is only useful if the work continues moving afterward.

That can include:

- Making scheduling changes
- Rebooking travel
- Coordinating personal logistics
- Gathering missing information
- Following up with owners
- Tracking commitments
- Updating stakeholders
- Preparing executive materials
- Monitoring deadlines
- Escalating new risks
- Closing completed loops

The Executive Partner owns the continuity between the decision and the outcome.

---

## 6. BRIEF THE FOUNDER

The founder receives a compressed view of the original activity:

- Decisions requiring their judgment
- Meaningful risks
- Recommendations
- Relevant deadlines
- Important commitments
- High-value context

Everything else remains handled, delegated, monitored, or intentionally deferred.

---

## Example: Travel Disruption

**Raw input:**  
Tonight's flight is canceled. The founder must attend an investor meeting tomorrow morning.

**AI assistance:**  
Identify alternative flights, timing, existing itinerary details, calendar conflicts, and related reservations.

**Human judgment:**  
Evaluate the viable options against founder preferences, arrival risk, tomorrow's commitment, and downstream effects.

**Execution:**  
Rebook the best viable flight, update ground transportation, resolve the dinner conflict, and update the calendar.

**Founder experience:**  
"Your flight was canceled. I've moved you to the best replacement option, updated transportation, and resolved the dinner conflict. Tomorrow's investor meeting is unaffected."

The founder experiences the solution, not every step required to create it.

---

## Why Human Review Stays in the Loop

Executive support contains information and relationships that cannot always be understood from individual messages alone.

A system may recognize that a meeting is marked "urgent." It may not know that:

- The founder has been trying to protect a relationship for months.
- A candidate is unusually important to the company's next stage.
- A technically open calendar block should remain protected.
- A request that appears routine carries unusual sensitivity.
- An "urgent" request can be handled entirely by someone else.

AI provides leverage.

Context and judgment determine how that leverage should be used.
