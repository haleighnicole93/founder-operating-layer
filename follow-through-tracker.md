# Follow-Through Tracker

The Executive Partner owns visibility across work that has been handled, delegated, or set in motion.

The objective is to prevent the founder from becoming the follow-up mechanism.

## Follow-Up Rules

### Don't wait for deadlines to fail

A deadline should have an escalation point before the actual failure point.

If a launch requires a decision by 10:00 AM, 10:01 AM is too late to discover the decision never happened.

### Follow the dependency, not just the task

"Finance is handling it" is not a completed outcome if vendor service is still at risk.

Track the work until the dependency is actually resolved.

### Escalate exceptions, not routine progress

The founder does not need repeated updates that everything is proceeding normally.

Escalate when:

- A decision is required
- A deadline is genuinely at risk
- New information materially changes the situation
- Authority is exceeded
- Relationship or business risk increases
- The original plan is no longer viable

### Close the loop

Completed work should produce a clear outcome.

Not:

> Flight issue handled.

But:

> Replacement flight confirmed, transportation updated, dinner moved, and tomorrow's investor meeting remains unaffected.

---

## Follow-Through Principle

> The founder should not have to remember who owes what, chase routine updates, or rediscover decisions that have already been made.

The system holds the follow-up so the founder doesn't have to.
